Why does it pass through itself?

A true Klein bottle cannot be embedded in ordinary three-dimensional space without self-intersection. The neck crosses the wall in this rendering, but those crossing sheets are not joined. The abstract surface is smooth and has no hole or rim.

Camera & keyboard

Drag to rotate through any angle. Shift-drag or right-drag to pan. Scroll to zoom. On touchscreens, one finger rotates; two fingers pan and pinch to zoom. Double-click to reset.

With the canvas focused: arrow keys rotate, +/− zoom, Space toggles rotation and H resets. Manual rotation stops automatic rotation.

Geometry, not a physics simulation

This is a fixed parametric surface, rendered with two-sided lighting. The visual cutaway and parameter grid are viewing aids. There are no forces, masses or time integration, so energy conservation is not applicable. The mesh is sampled directly from its formula and does not deform or accumulate numerical drift.
